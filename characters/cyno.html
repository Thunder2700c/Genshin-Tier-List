[ 01 // 7.0 META SHIFT: STELLAR-CONDUCT & LUNAR-CHARGED ELEVATION ]

The 7.0 Stellar-Conduct Driver Renaissance

In Version 7.0, Cyno achieves SS-Tier standing primarily as a premier rapid-fire Stellar-Conduct driver alongside Sandrone and Odette. His high attack speed and split ATK/EM scaling allow him to continuously trigger the new 7.0 reactions. Additionally, his compatibility with the upcoming Lunar-Charged archetype (Columbina + Ineffa) broadens his team versatility beyond legacy Dendro setups.

ROSTER ROLE & KEY PARTNERS

Primary: Stellar-Conduct Driver • Secondary: Lunar-Charged / Aggravate • Key Partners: Odette, Sandrone, Columbina.

[ 03 // PULL DECISION MATRIX ]

✅ PULL IF

  • You plan to unlock his C1 constellation (+20% Normal Attack SPD for 10s upon burst, refreshed on every Judication hit).
  • You want an on-field Electro driver to trigger continuous Stellar-Conduct discharges alongside Odette and Sandrone.
  • You are assembling Lunar-Charged reaction teams featuring Columbina, Ineffa, and off-field Hydro units.
  • You farm the 7.0 Disenchantment in Deep Shadows artifact set to maximize reaction motion value.

❌ SKIP IF

  • You lack a dedicated shielder or poise-support; getting staggered during his burst severely degrades personal DPS.
  • You do not run transformative reaction teams (raw Electro damage alone without Conduct/Quicken is insufficient).
  • You rely on short-duration supports (under 12s) that expire mid-combo and leave him unbuffed.
  • You cannot meet his 130%–150% Energy Recharge threshold to burst reliably every single rotation.

[ 06 // MECHANICAL EXECUTION: THE ENDSEER TIMING WINDOW ]

Judication Timing Loop

During his burst, an "Endseer" eye icon flashes periodically on screen. Triggering his Elemental Skill precisely during this window activates the Judication effect (Featherfall Judgment), unleashing 3 homing Duststalker Bolts and increasing Skill DMG by 35%.

Execution Phase Action Sequence Strategic Purpose
Initial Cast Skill (E) → Burst (Q) → Skill (E) Casting Skill immediately before Burst pre-funnels Electro energy particles, starting his burst rotation with partial recharge.
Judication Loop Normal Attacks → Timed Skill (E) on Eye Timing Skill casts exclusively with the eye flash procs Duststalker bolts and constantly refreshes his C1 +20% ATK Speed buff.
4pc Thundering Fury Rotation Q → E → N4E → Eye (E) → N4E → Eye (E) TF cooldown reduction allows weaving extra non-eye Skills, generating additional Electro particles and dropping ER needs to ~125%.
