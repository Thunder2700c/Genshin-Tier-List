⚠️ 7.0 Meta Shift: C1 Swap Flexibility & Quickbloom Uptime

In Version 7.0, Cyno's SS-Tier placement is powered by his C1 constellation enhancement. By providing a flat +200 EM to Cyno and transferring an additional +200 EM to the swapped-in teammate for the remainder of his burst window, C1 fixes his historical rigidity. Players are no longer penalized for cutting his burst short to refresh support buffs, turning him into a high-utility dual-carry anchor.

🧠 Rotation Timing & The 2-Endseer Optimization

During his burst, an "Endseer" eye icon appears on screen at set intervals. Timing his Elemental Skill (Chasmic Soulfarer) during this visual cue triggers the Judication state, firing 3 homing Duststalker bolts that deal 100% of his ATK as Electro damage and increase Skill damage by 35%.

Rotation Strategy Execution Window Practical Trade-offs & Support Uptime
Optimal 2-Endseer Swap (Recommended) Burst (Q) → Skill (E) → 1st Eye (E) → 2nd Eye (E) → Swap Out Limits on-field time to ~12 seconds. Captures 100% of Furina, Baizhu, and Nahida buffs before they expire, passing C1's +200 EM to the incoming support/sub-DPS.
Extended 3-Endseer Window Q → E → 1st Eye (E) → 2nd Eye (E) → 3rd Eye (E) Pushes field time to ~16–18 seconds. Third Endseer typically occurs after short buffs (Viridescent Venerer, Bennett, Yelan) have expired, causing a noticeable trailing DPS drop.
4pc Thundering Fury Loop E before Q → Q → E → N4E → Eye (E) → N4E → Eye (E) Cooldown reduction from TF allows casting extra Skills between Endseer procs, generating more Electro particles and reducing ER requirements by ~15%.

🌌 Constellation Scaling Breakdown

C0 Baseline hypercarry. High sustained output during burst, but constrained by 18-second field commitment and steep energy gates.
C1 Transformative SS-Tier Spike: Increases Normal Attack SPD by 20%, grants +200 EM during burst, and transfers +200 EM to the next character swapped in until the original burst duration concludes. Resolves rotational rigidity.
C2 Normal attack hits grant +10% Electro DMG Bonus for 4s (stacks up to 5 times for a total of +50% Electro DMG).
C4 Triggering Electro-related reactions during burst restores 3 Energy to all other party members (up to 5 times / 15 Energy per burst).
C6 After using his Skill during Endseer state, normal attacks unleash bonus Duststalker bolts without consuming extra cooldowns.

⚠️ Misleading Showcase Setups

  • • Trap: Running 100% ER and failing to recharge burst for Chamber 2 or Wave 2.
  • • Distortions: Relying on 10-second external buffs (VV Shred / Bennett) that expire mid-combo.
  • • Reality: Without C1 or high ER substats, dragging out Cyno's full 18s burst is a net team DPS loss.

FINAL VERDICT

Cyno achieves SS-Tier standing in Version 7.0 thanks to his transformative C1 constellation, which unlocks rotational freedom by sharing +200 EM with the party upon swapping out. When paired with premiere Quickbloom supports (Nahida, Furina, Baizhu) and managed within a disciplined 2-Endseer window, he delivers exceptional sustained single-target and multi-reaction DPS.
