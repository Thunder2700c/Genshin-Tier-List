✅ PULL IF

  • • You plan to unlock his C1 constellation (+20% Normal Attack SPD for 10s upon burst, refreshed on every Judication hit).

  • • You are assembling Lunar-Charged reaction teams featuring Columbina, Ineffa, and off-field Hydro units.

❌ SKIP IF

  • • You lack a dedicated shielder or poise-support; getting staggered during his burst severely degrades personal DPS.

  • • You rely on short-duration supports (under 12s) that expire mid-combo and leave him unbuffed.
  • • You cannot meet his 130%–150% Energy Recharge threshold to burst reliably every single rotation.
⚠️ 7.0 Meta Shift: Stellar-Conduct & Lunar-Charged Elevation

In Version 7.0, Cyno achieves SS-Tier standing primarily as a premier rapid-fire Stellar-Conduct driver alongside Sandrone and Odette. His high attack speed and split ATK/EM scaling allow him to continuously trigger the new 7.0 reactions. Additionally, his compatibility with the upcoming Lunar-Charged archetype (Columbina + Ineffa) broadens his team versatility beyond legacy Dendro setups.

🧠 Mechanical Execution: The Endseer Timing Window

During his burst, an "Endseer" eye icon flashes periodically on screen. Triggering his Elemental Skill precisely during this window activates the Judication effect (Featherfall Judgment), unleashing 3 homing Duststalker Bolts and increasing Skill DMG by 35%.

🌌 Constellation Scaling Breakdown

C0 Strong baseline driver, but strictly demands 130%–150% ER and interrupt protection to maintain full burst uptime.
C1 Ordinance: Unceasing Vigil (SS-Tier Spike): After using Burst, Normal ATK SPD increases by 20% for 10s. Triggering Judication during Skill refreshes this duration, maintaining permanent +20% ATK SPD throughout his burst.
C2 Ceremony: Homecoming of Spirits: Normal attacks grant +10% Electro DMG Bonus for 4s (stacks up to 5 times for a total of +50% Electro DMG).
C4 Austerity: Forbidding Guard: Triggering Electro reactions during burst restores 3 Energy to all other party members (up to 5 times / 15 Energy total per burst).
C6 Raiment: Just Scales: Burst cast or Featherfall Judications grant 4 stacks of "Day of the Jackal." Normal attacks consume stacks to fire bonus Duststalker Bolts.

✅ Realistic C1 / Deep Shadows Baseline

  • • Weapon: R1 Staff of the Scarlet Sands or R5 White Tassel.
  • • Artifacts: 4pc Disenchantment in Deep Shadows (65% CR / 185% CDMG, 420 EM, 140% ER, 1,800+ ATK).

⚠️ Misleading Legacy Showcase Traps

  • • Trap: Building 100% ER and failing to have burst ready for multi-wave Abyss spawns.
  • • Distortions: Forcing 18-second burst field time with short 10s buffers that leave him unbuffed.
  • • Reality: Without poise protection and proper ER, getting staggered ruins entire rotation DPS.
