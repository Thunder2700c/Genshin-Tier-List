[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The 7.0 Stellar-Conduct Driver Renaissance

In Version 7.0, Cyno achieves SS-Tier standing primarily as a premier rapid-fire Stellar-Conduct driver alongside Sandrone and Odette. His high attack speed and split ATK/EM scaling allow him to continuously trigger the new 7.0 reactions. Additionally, his compatibility with the upcoming Lunar-Charged archetype (Columbina + Ineffa) broadens his team versatility beyond legacy Dendro setups.

ROSTER ROLE & KEY PARTNERS

Primary Archetype: Stellar-Conduct Driver • Secondary: Lunar-Charged / Aggravate • Key Partners: Odette, Sandrone, Columbina.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Elemental Burst (Lv 10)
2. Elemental Skill (Lv 9+)
3. Normal Attack (Leave at Lv 1)

His Burst converts attacks into Pactsworn Pathclearer normal strikes which scale strictly off Burst talent levels. Base Normal Attack talent does not scale burst damage.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • High-Frequency Reaction Driver: Drives continuous Stellar-Conduct and Lunar-Charged reactions through rapid multi-hit polearm strikes.
  • Transformative C1 Spike: Permanent +20% Normal Attack Speed refreshed continuously on every Judication skill cast.
  • Dual Scaling Synergy: Fluidly converts Elemental Mastery into flat ATK and reaction bonus values via Scarlet Sands and passive kits.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • High Energy Recharge Gate: Strictly demands 130%–150% ER to cycle his 80-cost Elemental Burst reliably on multi-wave rotations.
  • Poise & Stagger Vulnerability: Lacks innate super-armor; taking heavy hits during his burst window causes massive DPS downtime without a shielder.
  • Extended Field-Time Lock: Long on-field duration can lead to DPS loss if paired with short-duration supports that expire mid-combo.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

SS TIER (C1) — THE HIGH-FREQUENCY CONDUCT KING

Cyno cements his place in the Version 7.0 SS-Tier meta as a premier high-frequency Stellar-Conduct and Lunar-Charged driver. His split ATK/EM scaling converts the Disenchantment in Deep Shadows set into overwhelming reaction damage, while his C1 guarantees permanent +20% ATK Speed across his entire burst window.
