[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The 7.0 Stellar-Conduct Renaissance

In Version 7.0, Cyno experienced the most dramatic meta resurgence in Genshin history. His 18-second burst window—previously penalized for outlasting support buffs—matches the continuous rotation cooldown of Odette and Sandrone. At C1 (Ordinance: Unceasing Vigil), his $+20\%$ Normal ATK SPD maximizes multi-target Stellar-Conduct discharge frequencies, outperforming all rival Electro on-field drivers.

THE ROTATIONAL UPTIME RULE

By pairing Cyno with high-duration 7.0 off-field teammates, every single Endseer (Duststalker Bolt) proc now triggers consecutive reaction cascades with zero downtime penalties.
