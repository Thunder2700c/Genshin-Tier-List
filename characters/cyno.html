⚠️ 7.0 Meta Shift: Stellar-Conduct Supercharges Cyno

In Version 7.0, Cyno's jump to SS-Tier is driven entirely by the Stellar-Conduct reaction. His rapid Pactsworn claw attack rate makes him the premier Electro driver for Odette's aura. Combined with his C1 (+200 EM self-buff and +200 EM transfer upon early swap), Cyno triggers continuous high-motion-value elemental discharges while removing his legacy rotational rigidity.

🧠 Rotation Timing & The 2-Endseer Optimization (Zajef Theorycraft)

In 7.0 Stellar-Conduct teams, dragging Cyno's burst out for the full 18 seconds is a net team DPS loss due to Odette, Sandrone, and support buffs expiring. The mathematical sweet spot is the 2-Endseer Swap.

Rotation Method Sequence Why It Works in 7.0 Meta
Optimal 2-Endseer Swap (Recommended) Q → E → 1st Eye (E) → 2nd Eye (E) → Early Swap Captures 100% of Odette's Pulsar field stacks and support shred within a compact 12–13s window, passing C1's +200 EM to the next sub-DPS.
Thundering Fury Loop E before Q → Q → E → N4E → Eye (E) → N4E → Eye (E) Cooldown reduction from 4pc TF allows extra Skill casts between eye intervals, generating extra particles and cutting ER needs by ~15%.
Extended 3-Endseer (Sub-Optimal) Q → E → 1st Eye → 2nd Eye → 3rd Eye Third Endseer falls outside support buff windows, dropping unbuffed un-shredded damage and extending overall rotation length.

🌌 Constellation Scaling Breakdown

C0 Solid baseline driver. Strong raw damage, but locked into full burst uptime commitments with high energy requirements.
C1 SS-Tier Transformative Spike: +20% Normal Attack SPD, +200 personal EM during burst, and transfers +200 EM to the next swapped-in character until the original burst timer ends. Solves all rotation penalties.
C2 Normal attack hits grant +10% Electro DMG for 4s (stacks 5 times for a total of +50% Electro DMG Bonus).
C4 Triggering Electro reactions during burst restores 3 Energy to all other party members (up to 5 times / 15 Energy total).
C6 After using his Skill in the Endseer state, normal attacks unleash additional Duststalker bolts without cooldown constraints.
