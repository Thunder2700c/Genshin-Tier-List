[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Unrivaled Lunar-Crystallize DPS Benchmark

With the addition of Linnea into the Geo roster, Zibai's DPS has skyrocketed, establishing the definitive new ceiling for Main DPS carries. Even without Linnea, Zibai surpasses the damage output of most meta teams while maintaining an exceptionally straightforward and comfortable rotation, free from the strict mechanical demands of units like Nefer.

PHASE SHIFT RADIANGE & LUNAR-CRYSTALLIZE

Zibai primarily casts her Elemental Skill to infuse attacks with Geo and accrue Phase Shift Radiance through Normal Attacks and Lunar-Crystallize triggers, culminating in the colossal frontloaded nuke: Spirit Steed's Stride.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Elemental Skill
2. Elemental Burst
3. Normal Attack

Elemental Skill (Heaven and Earth Made Manifest) is the highest priority as it governs the entire frontloaded damage of Spirit Steed's Stride. Elemental Burst (Tri-Sphere Eminence) follows for supplementary rotation damage. Normal Attack talent levels do not contribute meaningfully to her damage.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Pinnacle Lunar-Crystallize Carry: Redefines team DPS benchmarks with frontloaded DEF-scaling reaction nukes.
  • Smooth Rotation Flow: Simple, intuitive combo loop (`Skill → NA x4 → Special Skill`) with high comfort.
  • Innate Team Scaling: Hydro and Geo allies in party directly increase her personal Elemental Mastery and DEF.
  • Night Energy Recovery: Restores Elemental Energy automatically at night for exploration convenience.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Restrictive Teambuilding: Rigidly tethered to Hydro and Geo Moonsign characters (Columbina, Linnea, Illuga, Aino).
  • Contested Support Tax: Heavily competes for Columbina, who is in high demand across other top Lunar archetypes.
  • Zero Geo Goblet Value: Lunar Reactions do not scale with Elemental DMG% bonuses, making Geo DMG goblets useless.
