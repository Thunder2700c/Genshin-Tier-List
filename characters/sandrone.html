[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Benchmark Carry of Version 7.0

With the release of Odette and the evolution of the Stellar-Conduct reaction framework, Sandrone defines the offensive ceiling of Version 7.0. Her near-exponential scaling from Stellar Glimmer stacks and seamless Charged Attack cadence allow her to deliver the highest continuous DPS score in the game when paired with proper Electro batteries.

CORE MECHANICAL ADVANTAGE

Unlike traditional Cryo units locked to Freeze or Forward Melt, Sandrone’s damage output is elevated directly through teammate procs and A4 passive ATK-to-EM conversion, bypassing standard internal cooldown restrictions.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Normal / Charged Attack
2. Elemental Burst
3. Elemental Skill

Charged Attacks represent 65%+ of her total damage output during Fagio's Decoding Mode. Her Burst provides a massive rotation nuke, while Skill is used mainly for utility and Decoding Power reduction.
