[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Frontloaded Electro Plunging Dynamo

Varesa commands the battlefield as a high-mobility Electro catalyst hypercarry built around massive, frontloaded plunging attack motion values. Operating through Natlan's Nightsoul system, she generates points via Plunging Attacks and her Elemental Skill to enter the empowered Fiery Passion state. In this mode, she expends Nightsoul energy to execute the devastating Thunderous Tornado Eruption special plunge. Her self-launching kit provides airborne invulnerability frames during combat and thrives in dedicated Chevreuse Overload, Xianyun Hypercarry, and modern Lunar-Charged compositions.

FIERY PASSION & APEX DRIVE BURST PLUNGE LOOP

Accumulating 100 Nightsoul points or casting Elemental Burst (Guardian Vent!) activates Fiery Passion for 15s. During this window, her Plunging Attacks consume all points for heavy AoE Electro impacts, activating Apex Drive to immediately trigger her zero-cooldown special Burst plunge: Guardian Vent: Volcano Kablam.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Normal Attack
2. Elemental Burst
3. Elemental Skill

Normal Attack (By the Horns) takes crowning priority as it directly scales her core Plunging Attack shockwaves. Elemental Burst follows closely for its massive entry damage and special Volcano Kablam plunge multipliers, while Elemental Skill is leveled last for base rush damage and Nightsoul uptime.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • High Innate Plunge Scaling: Leverages top-tier base plunging multipliers with built-in airborne verticality to naturally evade incoming ground damage.
  • Effortless Self-Enabling: Initiates high-speed leaping combos through her own Charged Attack and Skill without being strictly tied to Xianyun's jump buff.
  • Unmatched Exploration Utility: Sudden Onrush and Nightsoul Transmission allow rapid traversal across land, sheer cliffs, water, and liquid phlogiston without fall damage.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • No Amplifying Multipliers: Lacks access to high-multiplier forward reactions like Vaporize or Melt, relying strictly on raw talent scalings and additive buffs.
  • Narrow Weapon Synergies: Heavily favors her signature catalyst Vivid Notions; most other 5-star catalysts only function as passive stat sticks.
  • Nightsoul Gauge Pacing at C0: Managing point generation and Burst energy without C2 requires strict rotation discipline to prevent downtime.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

A+ TIER — THE ACROBATIC ELECTRO POWERHOUSE

Varesa firmly locks down A+ Tier status as an acrobatic, frontloaded Electro plunging carry with an exceptionally high damage floor. While she lacks the forward-multiplying reactions of Pyro carries, her raw motion values, self-sufficient aerial combo loops, and massive synergy with Natlan supports make her an electrifying pickup, with C2 unlocking unlimited burst-plunge uptime.
