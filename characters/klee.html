[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Explosive Pyrotechnic Vanguard of Mondstadt

Klee operates as a high-density, frontloaded Pyro catalyst hypercarry distinguished by unmatched stagger potential, relentless heavy Pyro application, and team battery mechanics. While traditional forward-Vaporize rotations can struggle to maintain Hydro auras against her rapid attacks, Klee thrives in Chevreuse Overload, Mono-Pyro, and modern Hexerei rosters alongside Durin and Nicole. With her A1 granting free, empowered Charged Attacks and her iconic C2 shredding enemy DEF by 23%, she functions seamlessly as both a primary on-field blaster and a premier offensive enabler.

POUNDING SURPRISE & EXPLOSIVE SPARK CYCLES

Normal Attacks and Jumpy Dumpty hits have a 50% chance to grant an Explosive Spark, making the subsequent Charged Attack cost 0 Stamina and deal +50% DMG. Her Charged Attack CRIT hits further restore 2 flat Elemental Energy to all party members via her A2 passive.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Normal / Charged Attack
2. Elemental Skill
3. Elemental Burst

Normal Attack (Kaboom!) takes crowning priority to maximize the massive base motion values of her Explosive Spark-buffed Charged Attacks. Elemental Skill (Jumpy Dumpty) follows for multi-bounce burst and minefield creation, while Elemental Burst (Sparks 'n' Splash) provides autonomous continuous Pyro damage.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Stagger & Poise Domination: Charged Attacks and Jumpy Dumpty bombs deliver massive blunt impact, tossing light mobs and interrupting elite enemies easily.
  • Universal C2 DEF Shred: Her 23% DEF reduction is one of the rarest damage multipliers in Genshin, allowing her to function as a top-tier damage amp support.
  • Hexerei Renaissance: Gained massive meta revitalization in Version 7.0 through synergy with Durin, Nicole, and the dedicated Hexerei artifact set.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Short Attack Range & Fragility: Clunky short-range animations and low base defensive stats leave her heavily exposed without dedicated shields or poise support.
  • Overwhelming Pyro Application: Extremely dense Pyro output overwhelms Hydro auras, making consistent forward-Vaporize impractical outside of Mono-Pyro or Overload.
  • Demanding Animation Canceling: Extracting peak DPS requires mastering jump cancels and animation cancels to circumvent stiff casting frames.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

A TIER — THE SPARK KNIGHT OF FAVONIUS

Klee remains an explosive, mechanically engaging Pyro catalyst carry defined by high-impact poise breaking and relentless Pyro barrage output. While her short combat range and animation canceling curve require deliberate piloting, her game-changing C2 defense shred, battery passives, and modern Hexerei synergies ensure she commands enduring combat value in both carry and support enabler roles.
