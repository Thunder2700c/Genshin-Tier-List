⚠️ 7.0 Meta Shift: Stellar-Conduct Over Outdated Melt

In Version 7.0, Wriothesley's optimal archetype has transitioned from legacy Reverse-Melt into a dedicated Stellar-Conduct driver. Paired with off-field Electro triggers (Yae Miko, Beidou) and allies like Sandrone or Odette, his constant catalyst Cryo application sets off continuous reaction cascades rather than waiting on slow Pyro applications.
