[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Unstoppable C1 Cryo Juggernaut

At C0, Wriothesley is constrained by a strict $60\%$ HP gate to access his enhanced charged attacks. At C1 (Terror for the Evildoers), this restriction is completely removed—guaranteeing Rebuke: Vaulting Fist every 5-hit Normal Attack string alongside an extra $+200\%$ DMG multiplier. Combined with 7.0 HP fluctuation artifact sets, his rotational consistency and sustained single-target DPS reach absolute pinnacle thresholds.

THE C1 TRANSFORMATION RULE

C1 converts Wriothesley from a fragile high-risk melee brawler into a self-sustaining juggernaut that automatically heals $30\%$ Max HP every rotation cycle without needing dedicated shield babysitting.
