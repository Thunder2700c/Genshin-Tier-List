Pinnacle 7.0 Stellar-Conduct Driver

In Version 7.0, Wriothesley's optimal clearing archetype has shifted away from circle-bound Reverse-Melt setups into a high-frequency Stellar-Conduct driver. [ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Stellar-Conduct Mastery: High-frequency Cryo catalyst application triggers devastating sustained reactions under Odette's 7.0 aura.
  • Transformative C1 Spike: Completely removes the 5-second special Charged Attack cooldown restriction and extends skill duration effortlessly.
  • Zero Burst Reliance: Delivers full combat power via Normal/Charged Attack strings without being gated by Energy Recharge or cooldown timers.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • C0 Mechanical Lockout: At C0, dropping below 50% HP disables enhanced hits while exceeding 60% locks out special Charged Attack heals.
  • Limited Base AoE: Melee catalyst attack radius requires enemies to be tightly grouped via Anemo CC or mobile repositioning.
  • Legacy Melt Obsolescence: Circle-bound Bennett/Xiangling Melt setups struggle in multi-wave 7.0 Abyss compared to mobile Conduct teams.
