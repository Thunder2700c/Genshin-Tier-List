[ 03 // PULL DECISION MATRIX ]

✅ PULL IF

  • You plan to unlock his C1 constellation to completely remove the 5-second special charged attack restriction.
  • You are building 7.0 Stellar-Conduct teams with enablers like Odette, Sandrone, and off-field Electro triggers.
  • You want a relentless on-field catalyst normal attacker with virtually zero reliance on Burst cooldowns.
  • You enjoy dynamic melee brawling combos and high-poise single-target boss disruption.

❌ SKIP IF

  • You are strictly staying at C0 and dislike managing tight HP thresholds (dropping below 50% disables Stellar damage).
  • You need sweeping wide-room AoE clearing without grouping supports like Sucrose or Kazuha.
  • You already have an invested Cryo carry (Skirk/Ayaka) and do not plan to run Electro/Stellar-Conduct teams.
  • You rely on passive circle healers that prevent him from dipping below 60% HP at C0.

[ 06 // COMBO NUANCE & HP MANAGEMENT (C0 MATH) ]

Rotation Execution Mechanics

At C0, one full normal attack string drains 27% HP, while a Special Charged Attack heals 30% HP (+3% net positive per $N5C$). Uncontrolled $N5C$ spam causes Wriothesley to slowly overheal above 60% HP, locking him out of his passive. Additionally, his 3rd hit ($N3$) is a dedicated Stellar hit that must connect under buffs.

Combo Technique Input Sequence Strategic Purpose
$N1 \rightarrow E$ Opener Normal 1 → Skill (E) → Normal 2 → Normal 3 Casting Skill does not cancel the attack string. Opening with $N1$ makes the first hit after Skill $N2$, landing the buffed $N3$ Stellar hit instantly while maintaining exact HP neutrality across rotations.
$N4$-Dash Skip N1 → N2 → N3 → Dash → N5 Skips the double-hit $N4$ animation without dropping combo count, compensating for the $+4.5\%$ HP variance and balancing out HP consumption.
Emergency Boss Recovery Direct $N3C$ or $N1C$ If knocked by aggressive enemies below 50% HP (where hits lose Stellar status), immediately execute a Charged Attack to heal back into the 50%–60% operational zone.
