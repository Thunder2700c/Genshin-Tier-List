✅ PULL IF

  • • You want an on-field carry with unmatched AoE clear and built-in healing.
  • • You want high performance using only a craftable weapon (Prototype Amber).
  • • You need a hypercarry completely free from Bennett/Xingqiu circle dependencies.
  • • You want a unit capable of carrying Abyss chambers practically solo.

❌ SKIP IF

  • • You dislike channeled beam gameplay or find charging attacks repetitive.
  • • You strictly prefer fast-paced multi-character quickswap burst rotations.
  • • You refuse to run a shielder at C0 to manage knockback interruption.
  • • Your Abyss teams already comfortably clear with 36 stars.
⚠️ Account Redundancy Warning

Neuvillette dominates the On-Field Hydro Carry archetype. While he directly powercreeps older options like Kamisato Ayato in raw sustained damage, accounts that already run specialized reaction setups (such as Tartaglia International or Nilou Bloom) may see diminishing returns if their missing gaps are in other elements like Cryo or Dendro.

⚖️ Strengths, Weaknesses & Execution

🟢 STRENGTHS

  • • Continuous sweeping AoE Hydro laser with immense range.
  • • Completely self-sufficient HP recovery mechanic via Sourcewater Droplets.
  • • Outstanding performance with accessible craftable 4-star weapons.
  • • Scales purely on HP, making artifact progression straightforward.

🔴 WEAKNESSES

  • • Vulnerable to interruption during beam charge-up at C0 without a shielder.
  • • Requires triggering 3 distinct Hydro reactions at C0 for max passive stacks.
  • • Completely ineffective against Hydro-immune elemental shields.
  • • High demand for Furina and Kazuha to reach peak ceiling.

🌌 Constellation Scaling (C0 Benchmark)

C0 Complete baseline power. Exceptional damage output, but benefits from a shielder to prevent knockback.
C1 Major quality-of-life spike. Grants complete interruption resistance during beam and starts rotations with 1 free Draconic Glory stack (unlocks Hydro Resonance with Furina).

C6 Absorbs nearby droplets automatically to extend the laser beam indefinitely with extra torrents.
