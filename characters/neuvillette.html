[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Supreme Sustained Hydro Sovereign

Neuvillette stands as a premier sustained on-field Hydro hypercarry powered entirely by Max HP scaling and his signature Charged Attack beam, Equitable Judgment. By harvesting Sourcewater Droplets spawned from his Elemental Skill and Burst, Neuvillette bypasses charging windups to deliver massive, mobile 360-degree piercing Hydro AoE while continuously self-healing. Though modern endgame DPS checks increasingly favor frontloaded burst windows and dedicated Nod-Krai elemental reactions (such as Lunar-Bloom and Lunar-Charged), Neuvillette remains unmatched in rotational consistency, self-sufficiency, and general ease of execution.

EQUITABLE JUDGMENT & SOURCEWATER DROPLET LOOP

Consuming 3 Sourcewater Droplets instantly charges Neuvillette's beam for 3s of continuous AoE Hydro damage while restoring 48% Max HP. While casting above 50% HP, he drains his own health to trigger artifact passives and max out his A2 Hydro DMG bonus (+30%).

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Normal / Charged Attack
2. Elemental Burst
3. Elemental Skill

Normal Attack (As Water Finds a Whispering Level) directly dictates the scaling of Equitable Judgment and represents ~85% of his damage. Elemental Burst (O Tides, I Have Returned) is leveled second to maximize damage while generating 6 Droplets, followed by his Elemental Skill for 3 instant Droplets.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Infinite Piercing AoE: Equitable Judgment fires a massive Hydro torrent that penetrates all targets with 360-degree free aim and full movement mobility.
  • Unrivaled Self-Sufficiency: High-speed HP self-drain combined with Droplet absorption heals up to 48% Max HP per beam, eliminating dedicated healer reliance.
  • High F2P Weapon Floor: Scales extraordinarily well with accessible options like craftable Prototype Amber and R5 Battle Pass Sacrificial Jade.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Interruption Sensitivity at C0: Taking heavy attacks during Equitable Judgment cancels the beam and wastes Droplets unless protected by a shielder or C1.
  • Rainbow Reaction Lock: At C0, requires 3 distinct Hydro reactions to max out his A1 passive (160% multiplier), restricting pure Mono-Hydro lineups.
  • Sustained vs Frontloaded Checks: Longer damage delivery profile can feel slower against newer endgame boss timers that demand instant burst DPS.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

A TIER — THE TIMELESS HYDRO SOVEREIGN

Neuvillette remains the gold standard for reliable, high-floor sustained Hydro destruction. While evolving endgame metas emphasize frontloaded reaction bursts, Neuvillette's sweeping AoE coverage, self-healing independence, unmatched exploration hover utility, and colossal C1 value make him one of the most accessible and dependable carries in Genshin Impact history.
