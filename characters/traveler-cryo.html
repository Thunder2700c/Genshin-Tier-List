[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

Snezhnaya's Futureproofed F2P Powerhouse

The Cryo Traveler bears an exceptionally solid baseline and long-term relevance across the Snezhnaya meta landscape. Armed with access to both Stellar-Swirl and Stellar-Conduct, the story-acquired Exaiphanes Blade, and self-sufficient reaction triggers, this variant stands out as the most futureproofed element in the Traveler's kit.

SANDRONE F2P SIDEGRADE METRIC

From a raw damage and utility standpoint, the Cryo Traveler serves as an accessible free sidegrade to Sandrone. By matching her reaction frequencies and scaling effortlessly with F2P gear, they provide an unmatched entry point into Version 7.0 teamcrafting.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Elemental Burst
2. Normal Attack
3. Elemental Skill

Elemental Burst (Frostbound Javelin) takes the highest priority for its massive nuke scaling that consumes Frostglow stacks. Normal Attacks follow for infused Cryo slashes (buffed by 80% ATK under A1), while Elemental Skill is leveled last for aura deployment.

[ 09 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Exceptional Free-to-Play Value: Fully accessible 5★ unit with completely free story constellations and signature sword (Exaiphanes Blade).
  • High Stellar Scaling: Delivers immense single-target and multi-target damage as a Stellar-Glimmer reaction anchor.
  • Self-Sufficient Reaction Trigger: Can trigger reactions independently while providing non-overrideable Cryo infusion.
[ 10 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Stellar Glimmer Dependency: Loses significant damage ceiling when placed outside of dedicated 7.0 Stellar reaction archetypes.
  • Restricted Teammate Options: Maximizing potential heavily favors specific modern partners like Odette and Yae Miko.
  • Zero Cryo Goblet Value: Standard elemental damage cups fall behind pure ATK% scaling in Stellar-Glimmer calculation formulas.
