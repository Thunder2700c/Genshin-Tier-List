✅ PULL IF

  • • You need a top-tier single-target and multi-target Pyro carry with zero burst energy reliance.
  • • You want seamless normal attack mechanics that can switch targets without downtime.
  • • You already have or can build Chevreuse, Bennett, or Yelan.
  • • You appreciate high-risk, high-reward glass cannon combat styles.

❌ SKIP IF

  • • You already invested heavily in Mavuika, Hu Tao, or Yoimiya.
  • • You struggle with dodging without external healers (cannot receive team heals in combat).
  • • Your account is missing essential universal supports (Kazuha / Xilonen / Furina).
  • • You dislike managing the Bond of Life mechanic.
⚠️ Account Redundancy Warning

Already own Mavuika or an invested Hu Tao? Arlecchino provides virtually the same combat output and team role: an on-field Pyro carry designed to vaporize or overload boss encounters. While her uptime mechanics are more forgiving than Hu Tao, pulling her is a sidegrade rather than an account expansion if you already possess a built Pyro roster.

⚖️ Strengths, Weaknesses & Execution

🟢 STRENGTHS

  • • Zero Energy dependence for normal attack Pyro infusions.
  • • Exceptional single-target damage scaling that rivals peak C0 carries.
  • • Extremely strong in both Vaporize and Chevreuse Overload team archetypes.
  • • Infusion persists after swapping out, enabling flexible mid-rotation adjustments.

🔴 WEAKNESSES

  • • Cannot be healed by party members while in combat (relies solely on her own Burst).
  • • Lack of native interruption resistance at C0 necessitates a shielder against aggressive bosses.
  • • Heavy competition with Bennet and Xingqiu/Yelan from other team compositions.
  • • Direct role overlap with several existing top-tier Pyro options.

📸 Damage Showcase Reality Check

✅ Realistic C0 Abyss Baseline

  • • Weapon: R5 White Tassel / R1 Deathmatch.
  • • Artifacts: 4pc Gladiator or Whimsy (~70% CRIT / 160% CDMG, 2,100 ATK).
  • • Team: Bennett (C6/C1) + Xingqiu + Kazuha / Zhongli.
  • • Output: 40k–80k per normal attack hit, steadily tapering as Bond of Life expends.

⚠️ Misleading Showcase Setup (Screenshots)

  • • Weapon / Constellation: C6 R5 Crimson Moon's Semblance.
  • • Distortions: Food buffs + 200% CDMG artifact fish + Level 60 boss with -80% native RES.
  • • Reality: Millions of damage on a single Burst hit does not represent realistic sustained C0 normal attack rotations.

FINAL VERDICT

Arlecchino is one of the strongest and most comfortable sustained single-target carries in the game due to her non-existent energy needs and flexible swap times. However, because she occupies the crowded on-field Pyro carry niche, evaluate your current roster: if you already have Mavuika or Hu Tao built, she is a comfort luxury pull rather than an account necessity.
