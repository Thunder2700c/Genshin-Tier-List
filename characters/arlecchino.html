[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Crimson Moon's Sustained Pyro Hypercarry

Arlecchino functions as an on-field Pyro carry designed around high-frequency normal attacks empowered by the Bond of Life mechanic. By tagging enemies with Blood-Debt Directives via her Elemental Skill and harvesting them with Charged Attacks, she enters the Masque of the Red Death state. While the current meta emphasizes frontloaded burst cycles and dedicated Nod-Krai reactions (such as Lunar-Bloom and Lunar-Charged), Arlecchino's immense raw multipliers, self-buffs, and flexible team synergies allow her to deliver consistent high-tier output.

MASQUE OF THE RED DEATH & EXCLUSIVE SELF-SUSTAIN

When Bond of Life reaches $\ge 30\%$ Max HP, Arlecchino converts Normal, Charged, and Plunging attacks into non-overrideable Pyro DMG. She gains a flat 40% Pyro DMG Bonus in combat but is completely immune to external healing, relying solely on casting Balemoon Rising for emergency HP restoration.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Normal Attack
2. Elemental Skill
3. Elemental Burst

Normal Attack (Invitation to a Beheading) directly dictates the scaling of her Pyro-infused normal strikes and must be crowned first. Elemental Skill (All Is Ash) is leveled second for Directive scaling, while Elemental Burst (Balemoon Rising) serves as an emergency self-heal and cooldown reset.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Self-Sufficient Hypercarry: High raw scalings and innate +40% Pyro DMG Bonus allow her to run effectively with 3 buffer supports or a shielder.
  • Versatile Team Archetypes: Flexible combat driver across Vaporize, Forward Melt, Chevreuse Overload, and Mono-Pyro setups.
  • Exploration Flight Utility: Charged Attack hold provides high-speed aerial hover sprinting to traverse terrain and water gaps.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • High-Risk Self-Sustain: Cannot be healed by team healers in combat; surviving requires managing Bond of Life for Burst heals or running a shielder.
  • Interruption Sensitivity: Without C1 or dedicated shielders, taking hits during normal attack combos causes significant DPS loss.
  • Meta Reaction Disconnect: Lacks direct scaling with Nod-Krai elemental reactions (Lunar-Bloom / Lunar-Charged), requiring hyperinvestment to match modern ceilings.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

A TIER — THE SUPREME BALEMOON HARBINGER

Arlecchino remains a mechanically rewarding, high-ceiling Pyro hypercarry centered around her unique Bond of Life engine. While evolving meta landscapes favor frontloaded reaction setups, her high raw normal attack scaling, diverse team options across Melt, Vape, and Overload, and massive C1/C2 spikes ensure she remains a formidable on-field carry.
