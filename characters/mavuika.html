✅ PULL IF

  • • You lack a dominant 5-star on-field DPS for high-floor Abyss clearing.
  • • You need flexible off-field Pyro application with huge frontloaded burst nukes.
  • • You want a premier anchor for Natlan's Nightsoul burst mechanic.
  • • You already have or plan to build Xilonen and Citlali.

❌ SKIP IF

  • • You already own a fully built Arlecchino or Hu Tao team and lack other elements.
  • • Your account lacks foundational universal supports (Xilonen, Furina, Kazuha).
  • • You dislike vehicle combat maneuvers or driving-based mechanical execution.
  • • You are basing your pull decision solely on 2M+ screenshot showcase clips.
⚠️ Account Redundancy Warning

Already own a built Arlecchino or Hu Tao? Mavuika fulfills the same primary on-field Pyro carry slot on Floor 12. While her off-field flexibility gives her an edge over pure hypercarries, pulling her will not solve missing elemental coverage on accounts lacking Cryo, Hydro, or Dendro damage dealers.

FINAL VERDICT

Mavuika sets the benchmark for Pyro DPS and team synergy in Version 7.0. Her dual ability to output overwhelming on-field damage while retaining off-field sub-DPS utility makes her an invaluable anchor for new and developing rosters, though existing accounts with peak Arlecchino or Hu Tao teams should carefully weigh role redundancy.
