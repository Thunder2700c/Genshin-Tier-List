🔴 Role Redundant (Luxury)

✅ PULL IF

  • • You lack a dominant 5-star on-field DPS for Abyss clearing.
  • • You need flexible off-field Pyro application with massive burst capability.
  • • You want a top-tier driver for Natlan's Nightsoul burst mechanic.
  • • You already have or plan to build Xilonen and Citlali.

❌ SKIP IF

  • • You already own a fully built Arlecchino or Hu Tao team.
  • • Your account lacks foundational supports (Xilonen, Furina, Kazuha).
  • • You dislike driving-based combat modes or vehicle combat mechanics.
  • • You are basing your pull decision solely on 2M+ screenshot showcases.
⚠️ Account Redundancy Warning

Already own a built Arlecchino or Hu Tao? Mavuika competes for the exact same on-field Pyro hypercarry slot on Floor 12. While her off-field flexibility gives her an edge over pure hypercarries, pulling her will not solve missing elemental coverage on accounts lacking Cryo, Hydro, or Dendro damage dealers.

⚖️ Strengths, Weaknesses & Execution

🟢 STRENGTHS

  • • Industry-leading burst scaling and sweeping AoE attacks.
  • • Dual-role capability: can act as pure on-field carry or off-field enabler.
  • • Independent of Bennett's circle-impact restriction during vehicle state.
  • • Night Soul traversal and combat mobility reduce field downtime.

🔴 WEAKNESSES

  • • Heavy reliance on Nightsoul points from teammates to maximize burst.
  • • Vehicle turning radius takes adjustment against hyper-mobile enemies.
  • • High competition for premier off-field supports (Xilonen, Citlali, Furina).
  • • Overlaps heavily with existing high-investment Pyro rosters.

SS TIER

FINAL VERDICT

Mavuika sets the benchmark for Pyro DPS and team synergy in Version 7.0. Her dual ability to output overwhelming on-field damage while retaining off-field sub-DPS utility makes her an invaluable anchor for new and developing rosters, though existing accounts with peak Arlecchino or Hu Tao teams should carefully weigh role redundancy.
