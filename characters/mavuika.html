[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Pinnacle Pyro Standard of Version 7.0

Mavuika serves as the definitive offensive centerpiece for Natlan and Version 7.0 teamcrafting. Her Elemental Burst possesses the single highest raw motion value scaling in the Pyro roster, while her Elemental Skill provides permanent 100% uptime off-field Pyro coordinated strikes that consume zero energy, operating entirely on Fighting Spirit.

THE UNIVERSAL ARCHON PRINCIPLE

Even on accounts that already possess hypercarries like Arlecchino or Hu Tao, Mavuika delivers irreplaceable account value by serving as the ultimate off-field enabler for Melt, Overload, and Stellar-Conduct compositions.
