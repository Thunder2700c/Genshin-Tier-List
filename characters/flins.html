[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Pinnacle Lunar-Charged Driver of Version Luna V

Flins functions as a premier on-field hypercarry centered around high-frequency Lunar-Charged procs. His unique passive converting ATK into Elemental Mastery, combined with the Night of the Sky's Unveiling artifact set, eliminates stat-building bottlenecks. By weaving his special Elemental Skill Northland Spearstorm and mini-burst Thunderous Symphony, Flins deals massive frontloaded and sustained damage that scales directly off both his own and his allies' investment.

CRITICAL-STRIKING LUNAR-CHARGED MECHANICS

Flins elevates standard Electro-Charged into Lunar-Charged, converting every 100 ATK into +0.7% base reaction DMG (up to +14%) and elevating team Moonsign by +1 level, allowing reaction procs to critically strike.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Elemental Burst
2. Elemental Skill
3. Normal Attack

Elemental Burst (Ancient Ritual: Cometh the Night) and its special variant Thunderous Symphony represent the majority of Flins' rotational damage. Elemental Skill enters the Manifest Flame state to enable coordinated strikes, while basic Normal Attacks can be left uninvested.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • High damage multipliers on Lunar-Charged procs that uniquely benefit from CRIT Rate and CRIT DMG scaling.
  • Innate ATK-to-EM conversion passive allows stacking pure ATK% main stats while maximizing reaction value.
  • Elevates party Moonsign by +1 level, unlocking high-tier reaction damage states for all teammates.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Strictly tethered to Lunar-Charged archetypes; suffers severe DPS drop-offs in Quicken, Hyperbloom, or Overload setups.
  • High Energy Recharge dependency (130%–140%) requiring a secondary Electro battery to cycle bursts reliably.
  • Electro DMG% goblets are suboptimal due to reaction formulas favoring raw ATK% scaling.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

SS TIER — THE SUPREME LUNAR-CHARGED JUGGERNAUT

Flins defines the offensive ceiling for Electro-Hydro reaction archetypes in Version Luna V. His ability to convert standard Electro-Charged into critical-striking Lunar-Charged procs, combined with innate dual ATK/EM scaling, makes him an exceptional standalone hypercarry that requires zero constellation investment to reach peak combat output.
