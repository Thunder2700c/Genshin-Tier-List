[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Multi-Element PHEC Vanguard of Mondstadt

Varka establishes a distinct identity as an Anemo carry that bypasses standard Elemental Mastery stacking in favor of pure ATK scaling. Operating as a flexible PHEC (Pyro/Hydro/Electro/Cryo) driver, his damage is concentrated heavily in his Elemental Skill state. By dual-wielding claymores during Sturm und Drang, he dishes out rapid elemental-infused slashes that scale off his party composition and Hexerei team synergies.

DAWN WIND'S MARCH & 220% MULTIPLIER CAP

Every 1,000 ATK grants +10% Anemo and PHEC DMG (up to +25%). Pairing at least 2 Anemo allies and 2 matching PHEC elemental units elevates all Sturm und Drang motion values to a colossal 220% scaling.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Elemental Skill
2. Elemental Burst
3. Normal Attack

Elemental Skill (Windbound Execution) is the primary damage source for all rotations. Burst is largely a DPS loss due to long cast animations, and basic Normal Attack talent levels do not scale his empowered state.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Fluid and intuitive combat rhythm focusing entirely on Elemental Skill reactivations and dual-blade normal attack strings.
  • Exceptional versatility across Mono-Pyro, Hydro, Electro, and Cryo team setups without requiring Elemental Mastery.
  • Superior open-world traversal leap capable of crossing massive chasms and scaling sheer elevation effortlessly.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Rigid team restrictions: Demands exactly 1 Anemo, 2 matching PHEC units, and 1 Hexerei ally to activate maximum passive multipliers.
  • Heavy reliance on signature weapon (Gest of the Mighty Wolf); generic claymores result in noticeable DPS drop-offs.
  • Elemental Burst is generally a DPS loss compared to continuous on-field Skill cycling due to animation time.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

S TIER — THE KNIGHT OF BOREAS PHEC JUGGERNAUT

Varka redefines Anemo carry gameplay by shifting away from transformative Swirl mechanics into a pure ATK-scaling PHEC infusion juggernaut. While bound by strict team architecture requirements (2 Anemo + 2 matching PHEC + Hexerei), his skill-focused dual-wielding combat loops and immense open-world mobility make him a powerful and adaptable carry.
