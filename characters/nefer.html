[ 01 // META PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW ]

The Apex On-Field Lunar-Bloom Carry

Nefer enters the meta as the definitive on-field driver for Lunar-Bloom teams. Her kit uniquely absorbs field Dendro Cores, converting them into Seeds of Deceit under the Ascendant Gleam Moonsign to fuel her high-damage Phantasm Performance Charged Attacks. Because Lunar-Bloom can critically strike, her output rivals top-tier hypercarries when paired with her core support, Lauma.

CORE ABSORPTION & SUSTAIN RULE

By consuming Dendro Cores as fuel rather than letting them detonate on the ground, Nefer completely mitigates Bloom self-damage, freeing up team slots from requiring strict defensive circle healers.

[ 02 // TALENT PRIORITY ]

Skill Leveling Order

1. Elemental Skill
2. Elemental Burst
3. Normal Attack

Elemental Skill (Senet Strategy) takes top priority as it governs the Shadow Dance state and Phantasm Performance motion values. Burst follows for frontloaded AoE damage, while basic Normal Attacks can be left uninvested.

[ 10 // STRENGTHS ]
  • Critical Striking Lunar-Bloom: Converts standard Bloom reactions into a higher-tier archetype that scales with CRIT multipliers.
  • Self-Sustaining Mechanics: Core absorption negates Bloom explosion damage, reducing reliance on dedicated defensive healers.
  • Streamlined Stat Building: Scales strictly off Elemental Mastery and CRIT stats with near-zero ATK dependency.
[ 11 // LIMITATIONS & TRAPS ]
  • Strict Support Requirement: Reaching peak damage ceiling heavily depends on having Lauma to unlock Ascendant Gleam.
  • Anti-Synergy with Rapid Bloom Triggers: Cannot be paired with units like Nilou, as rapid detonations steal cores needed for her Charged Attacks.
  • Zero Dendro DMG Goblet Value: Standard Dendro cups fall far behind pure EM goblets in Lunar-Bloom damage calculations.
[ 12 // FINAL VERDICT ]

SS TIER — THE SUPREME LUNAR-BLOOM CARRY

Nefer delivers extraordinary transformative reaction DPS in Version Luna VI. While her performance is tightly coupled with Lauma, her ability to critically strike with Lunar-Bloom and absorb field cores without taking self-damage makes her the premier Dendro carry of the Nod-Krai era.
